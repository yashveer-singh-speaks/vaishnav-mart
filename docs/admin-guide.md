# Owner's Plain-English Guide to Vaishnava Mart

Welcome to your Vaishnava Mart Super Admin Dashboard. 

This guide is written in simple, straightforward language. You do not need to understand code, databases, or technical jargon to run your online store.

---

## 1. How to Open the Owner Dashboard

1. Open your website on your computer or mobile phone.
2. Scroll to the very bottom row of the footer (below the copyright and links).
3. In the bottom row, you will see a subtle email icon: **📧**
4. Click that icon. It will open the **Owner Login** page.
5. Enter your temporary owner email and password, then click **Enter Admin Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Understanding the Five Main Dashboard Cards

On the main dashboard screen, you will see five large cards. Each card represents one basic business action:

### 1. EDIT (Pencil Icon)
- **What it does:** Change anything already appearing on the website.
- **How to use it:**
  - Click **EDIT**, then click **Home Page**.
  - You will see the sections of your website arranged like **Lego blocks** (Hero, Why Vaishnava Mart Exists, Purity Story, etc.).
  - Click **Edit** on any block to change the heading text, paragraph, or picture.
  - Use the **Up** and **Down** arrows to change the order in which blocks appear.
  - When you are happy with your changes, click **Publish Changes**. Your live website will update immediately.

### 2. ORDERS (Package Icon)
- **What it does:** View and manage customer orders.
- **How to use it:**
  - When a customer orders, their order appears at the top of this list.
  - Click an order to see the customer's name, phone number, delivery address, and the exact items they bought.
  - As your kitchen team prepares the order, click **Mark Packed**, **Mark Shipped**, or **Mark Delivered** to keep the customer updated on their timeline.

### 3. USERS (People Icon)
- **What it does:** View your registered customers.
- **How to use it:**
  - Search any customer by name, phone number, or email.
  - Click on their name to see how many orders they have placed with you and their saved delivery addresses.

### 4. CART USERS (Cart Icon)
- **What it does:** View customers who added products to their cart but did not finish ordering.
- **How to use it:**
  - This screen shows customers with unfinished carts and the value of the items they were considering.
  - If the customer provided a phone number, you can click the **WhatsApp** button. This automatically opens a polite WhatsApp message offering assistance.

### 5. ADD (Plus Icon)
- **What it does:** Add new items to your business.
- **Options inside:**
  - **Add Product:** A 9-step guided assistant to launch a new spice blend, dry fruit mix, or premix. You enter the title, price, photos, and descriptions step by step.
  - **Add Category:** Create a new collection (e.g., "Navratri Fasting Foods").
  - **Add Section:** Add a new chapter block to any page.

---

## 3. Helpful Tips for Best Results

- **Photos:** Always use high-quality square (1:1) photos for products and wide (16:9) photos for page banners.
- **Stock Warnings:** If a product runs low on stock, you will see an alert on your dashboard so your team can mill fresh batches before stock runs out.
- **Safe Editing:** Changing sections saves a **Draft** first. Nothing goes live on the public website until you click **Publish Changes**.
